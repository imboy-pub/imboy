IMBoy Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how IMBoy collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the IMBoy application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account profile information (for example: account identifier, nickname, avatar).
  • Message metadata and service logs needed for message delivery, reliability, and security.
  • Device and network information used for login security, abuse prevention, and diagnostics.
  • Optional content you choose to upload (for example: images, files, voice messages).

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide chat, account, and notification functionality.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Improve product quality, performance, and stability.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Retention

Data retention depends on service purpose and legal obligations. When an account deletion request is completed, account data is removed or anonymized according to applicable policy and law.

4. Your Rights

  • You can request account deletion from inside the app.
  • If you cannot access the app, use the external account deletion channel below.

Account deletion instructions: /account-deletion

5. Contact

For privacy requests, contact: support@imboy.pub

IMBoy Account Deletion

Last updated: February 20, 2026

Delete account in app

  1. Open IMBoy.
  2. Go to Settings and open Delete account.
  3. Read the notice and submit the deletion request.

A deletion request enters a cooling-off period (60 days by default). You can cancel the pending request from the app while it is in the cooling-off period.

Delete account without app access

If you cannot log in to the app, send an account deletion request to support@imboy.pub.

What happens to your data

Deleted after the cooling-off period: your profile and account, sessions and devices, contacts and social graph, messages stored on our servers (end-to-end encrypted copies on other people's devices are outside our control), moments, channel subscriptions, AI/Bot agents, push tokens and E2EE key material.

Ownership first: groups, workspaces or channels you own are transferred to a remaining admin/member where possible; otherwise they are closed.

Retained (security and legal reasons, identity anonymised where applicable): operation and security audit logs, and financial/payment records required by accounting rules.

Balance gate: account deletion completes only when your wallet balance is zero. Withdraw or explicitly forfeit the balance first.

Check your deletion status

Sign in to IMBoy and open Settings → Delete account to see the current status (pending / cancelled / completed), the requested time and the expected completion date.

Delete account without app access

If you cannot log in to the app, send an account deletion request to support@imboy.pub.

Please include your account identifier and proof of ownership so the support team can verify and process the request.

What data is deleted

  • Account profile and account access credentials.
  • User-generated data associated with your account, subject to legal and security retention requirements.

What data may be retained

  • Operational and security logs required for fraud prevention, legal compliance, and dispute handling.
  • Data retained for a limited period where required by law or for security investigations.
